In modern cloud data centers, accurately predicting job failures before they occur is essential for ensuring system reliability, availability, and efficiency. To address this challenge, researchers have progressively developed machine learning and deep learning techniques that examine cloud logs to identify patterns linked to such failures. To surpass the prediction accuracy of earlier models, this paper presents the Multilayer Multi-Prediction Framework (MMPF), a novel approach specifically designed to enhance failure prediction accuracy. The framework is divided into two distinct layers. The first layer aims to detect job failures using a fine-tuned voting mechanism, while the second layer identifies the specific failure type.
